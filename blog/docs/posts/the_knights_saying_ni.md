# The knights who say NI

With currently happening corporate GenAI frenzy, search engines have diminished in value for queries which are any bit beyond the general or mainstream content.  
I tried today looking up some tech topic - I got a bunch of links with generated irrelevant bullshit preceding self-advertisement of the page owning startup.

Now, looks like we have to rely on the old way of mouth-to-ear recommendations to note down authors of useful, insightful or delightful content. 

So I am going to have a page with links to brain-crafted content I went across and liked. 

P.S. You've probably got it, but for just in case - NI in the title of this post stands for NAtural Intelligence.
