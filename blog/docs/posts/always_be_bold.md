# Always be bold

The sermon in one TV-show was about faith.
A catchy statement was about true faith staying strong and never fades in difficult
times or times of trial and ordeal.

That's relevant to the faith on yourself as well.
When you are down, the faith in yourself is something you need, and something you tend to loose,
but remember - you had it, now you stick to it, because that is the faith - sticking to it no matter what.
