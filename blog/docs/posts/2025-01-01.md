# diary record

A cognitive bias is an amusing thing to observe - when struggling with the problem for a while, having the documentation in front of your eyes, with the simple explanation given in plain English - you suddenly get it and see that all the complexity was just your own overthinking induction. Not quite an aha-moment you would look for, but still there is fun in it.
